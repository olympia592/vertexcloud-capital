FAQ

Questions we hear most

How much do I need to start?

You can begin with as little as $10 for crypto, $20 for ETFs and $100 for farm projects.

Can I lose money?

Yes. All investments carry risk and you can get back less than you invest. Each product shows a Low, Medium or High risk label.

How do farm and property returns work?

The expected return and term are shown before you invest. Payout happens at the end of the term, or earlier if you redeem.

How do I withdraw?

Open your wallet, choose Withdraw and enter an amount. Your bank receives it after our team confirms the request, usually within one working day.

How do I install the app?

On your phone, tap Download / Install app in the menu. On Android use Install app or Add to Home screen; on iPhone use Share then Add to Home Screen.
